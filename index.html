What is Chiefly?

Chiefly is an AI-powered leadership coach built for military and civilian leaders. Talk to your AI Chief out loud, capture meeting notes, and keep a decision journal that sharpens your judgment over time.

Key features

Voice coaching with natural conversation and automatic barge-in. AI meeting notes with post-meeting analysis. Decision journal with reflection prompts. Role-specific reference library for NCOs, officers, commanders, managers, and executives. Insights dashboard covering monthly, quarterly, and annual views.
