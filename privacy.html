Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Chiefly ("we", "us", the "app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Chiefly mobile application. Chiefly tracks what you commit to and whether you follow through. The voice coach is one input to that system, not the product. Chiefly is built and operated by AnchorForged (Tim Vaughan).

Information we collect

Account information

When you sign in using Apple, Google, or Microsoft, we receive your name and email address from the identity provider. We store these in Supabase (our backend database) so we can associate your account with commitments, decision journal entries, and coach conversations. Meeting notes are stored on your device, not in that account database.

Leadership profile

You tell us your leadership type (for example, military NCO, officer, or civilian manager) so follow-through context and reference material match your role. This is stored with your account.

Coach conversations

When you use the voice coach, the text of that conversation is sent to OpenAI via our backend so the coach can respond. Voice audio is transcribed on your device using Apple speech APIs, or sent to OpenAI Whisper for transcription, and is not stored after transcription. The coach is one voice inside Chiefly. It is not a separate product, and those conversations are not used to train models.

Meeting notes

When you record a meeting, audio is transcribed and sent to OpenAI for analysis. The resulting meeting note is stored locally on your device. Raw audio is not stored after transcription.

Decision journal entries

Journal entries you create are stored in Supabase associated with your account so you can access them across sessions.

Subscription information

Subscription state is managed by RevenueCat and Apple. We receive your subscription status (active, expired, trial) but never see your payment details, card numbers, or Apple ID password. Free includes the tracking loop. Pro is the optional paid depth plan, billed by Apple.

Diagnostic data

We use Sentry to capture crash reports and error logs. These may include device model, iOS version, app version, and a stack trace, but do not include the contents of your conversations, journal entries, or meeting notes.

How we use your information

  • Track commitments, decisions, and follow-through
  • Capture meetings in Note Taker and produce summaries
  • Respond when you choose to use the voice coach
  • Keep account data available across app sessions
  • Process and verify your subscription
  • Diagnose crashes and improve app stability
  • Respond to your support requests

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your coach conversations, journal entries, meeting notes, or other content to train AI models.

Third-party services

Chiefly relies on the following processors:

  • Supabase - account data, commitments and journal entries, authentication
  • OpenAI - coach responses and meeting analysis (gpt-4o, Whisper)
  • RevenueCat - subscription management
  • Apple / Google / Microsoft - sign-in (only when you choose that provider)
  • Sentry - crash and error reporting

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We only send the minimum data needed for the feature to work.

Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Settings - Delete Account inside the app, which removes your profile and associated data from our servers. Meeting notes stored on your device are removed when you delete the app.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correct inaccurate information, or delete your account by emailing us at the address below. If you are in the EU, UK, or California, you have additional rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA including the right to object to processing and the right to data portability. We will respond to verified requests within 30 days.

Children

Chiefly is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all network traffic and encrypted storage for account data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the app or via the email address associated with your account.

Contact
