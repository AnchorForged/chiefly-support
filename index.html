The accountability loop

  1. 1Said
  2. 2Captured
  3. 3Tracked
  4. 4Followed up
  5. 5Outcome

You make the commitment. Note Taker captures the meeting. Action Tracker holds the promise. Chiefly asks if you followed through. The weekly summary and decision journal show the outcome. The voice coach is one input to that loop, not the product.

Free runs the loop. Pro adds depth.
